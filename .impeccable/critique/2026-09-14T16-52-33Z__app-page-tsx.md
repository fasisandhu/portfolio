## Design Specificity Verdict

**Yes. It reads as vibe-coded — 5 of 6 known markers, spanning two AI clusters at once.**

| Marker | Present | Evidence |

| Section numbers (01/02/03) | no | Genuinely avoided |

The brief pinned "near-black, one teal accent" — the most-generated colour direction of the last two years — before a pixel was drawn. But the brief specified colour only. It did not ask for 11px letterspaced mono labels, hairlines to the margin, or the owner's name in JetBrains Mono. Roughly a third of the generated read is the brief; two thirds is the execution.

Key measurement: 118 elements render in monospace against 54 in sans. Two-thirds of the page is mono. Name, job title, companies, cities, university, "Dean's List all 8 semesters" — none is code, data, or measurement. Monospace as a costume is the loudest single tell.

**Deterministic scan:** CLI detector 0 findings, exit 0, across 16 files under app/ and components/ — clean. In-page overlay (live-server on :8400) found 6: 2x tight-leading, 3x line-length (90–107 chars/line vs 65–75 target), 1x all-caps-body.

**False positives (synthesizer's call):** both tight-leading findings are overruled — 1.03 line-height on a 54px display headline and 1.10 on display-sm are correct display typography; that rule targets body copy. The three line-length findings are real. The all-caps-body hit on "fitness ops · full-stack portal" is a judgment call but connects to the real mono-costume finding.

**Verified cascade bug (detector caught what design review could not):** Tailwind v4 emits text-accent / text-text inside @layer utilities; .label in globals.css is unlayered. Unlayered rules beat layered ones regardless of specificity, so .label's color: var(--dim) always wins. Confirmed via getComputedStyle: the "Open to remote roles" line, every h2 section heading, and the current-role date "Jul 2025 — Present" all compute to rgb(122,130,141) instead of teal/bright. 15 of 44 .label elements carry a dead override. Accent appears twice in the desktop first viewport, once on mobile.

## Overall Impression

The content is authored for this person. The design is authored for nobody. Strip the strings and what remains is one column, six sections, each identical: hairline, 11px mono label, rule to the margin, content. Seven instances of one device, and it is the page's only device. Swap in a designer's or a PM's projects, change the hex, and nothing breaks — the definition of category-interchangeable. A recruiter cannot read content.ts.

## What's Working

1. **Published demo credentials** (components/DemoLogin.tsx) — copy-to-clipboard fields inches from the Live demo button. Traceable directly to the positioning, and not a pattern generated portfolios produce, because a model has no credentials to publish. Converts an unverifiable claim into a two-second check. Build details are good: select-all fallback, role="status" announcement, timer cleared on unmount.
2. **Browser frame whose address bar is the real deployed host** (Work.tsx + .frame). A decorative browser mockup is a cliché; one where the URL is a checkable fact and the chrome is the anchor is an argument. Hover uses a real offset-and-blur shadow, not the coloured halo the craft floor refuses.
3. **Two-tone headline split** (taglineLead muted / taglineClaim bright). The only place type does work instead of furniture, and it survives into the OG card.

## Minor Observations

- OG card — which PRODUCT.md calls "the true first impression" — renders in a font that appears nowhere on the site: opengraph-image.tsx sets fontFamily "sans-serif" with no fonts array, so Satori falls back to its bundled default. The most-seen artifact is unbranded.
- Contrast is genuinely good: body 16.53:1, muted 6.42:1, dim 5.13:1, accent 11.90:1, accent-button ink 11.42:1. Accessibility is not the problem.
- h1 renders at 16px — smaller than several h3s and than body text. The 54.4px statement is a p.
- Same visual device carries three semantics: h2 in SectionHead, h3 in Background ("Résumé"), bare span in Background ("Certifications"). h3 "Résumé" renders 11px while sibling h3s render 18px.
- Experience ordering: Programmers Force (Aug–Dec 2024) listed third though it starts after Digitalytics (Mar 2024).
- Bullet markers are 10px hairlines at --line-strong (1.42:1) — effectively invisible.
- .display caps at 54.4px against a 96px craft ceiling, at weight 600 of a face that reaches 700.
- Footer reads "NEXT.JS · VERCEL" — stack-bragging as the last thing a reader sees.
- Design-rationale comments in globals.css and Rail.tsx read as a model narrating its own taste to anyone who opens the repo.
- Genuinely good craft worth keeping: font-synthesis-weight: none, the prefers-reduced-motion block, ::selection theming, and the single :focus-visible rule (verified teal outline on first three tab stops at both viewports).
- No horizontal overflow at either breakpoint; 0 overflowing elements.
